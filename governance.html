How VerDex Governance Works

VerDex uses a token-weighted on-chain governance model inspired by Compound and Uniswap. Every VDX token equals one vote.

01

Acquire VDX

Buy VDX on VerDex or earn it by providing liquidity. Your balance = your voting power.

02

Delegate

Self-delegate to vote directly, or assign your power to a trusted community representative.

03

Propose

Any wallet with ≥100K VDX can submit a proposal. Proposals go on-chain and enter a 72-hour vote window.

04

Vote

Cast FOR, AGAINST, or ABSTAIN during the voting period. Votes are weighted by your VDX balance at proposal creation block.

05

Quorum

10% of total VDX supply (100M VDX) must vote for the result to be binding. Below quorum = proposal fails.

06

Timelock

Passed proposals queue for 48 hours before execution. This gives the community time to review and exit if needed.

Proposal Lifecycle

1
DraftProposer composes and submits the proposal on-chain. Requires 100K VDX balance.
2
Active Voting72-hour window. VDX holders cast FOR / AGAINST / ABSTAIN. Voting power locked at proposal snapshot block.
3
Passed / FailedFOR > AGAINST AND quorum met = Passed. Otherwise = Failed. ABSTAIN votes count toward quorum but not outcome.
4
Timelock (48h)Passed proposals sit in the timelock contract for 48 hours. Anyone can read and react before execution.
5
ExecutedThe timelock executor calls the on-chain function. For signal proposals, the team implements manually after the vote.

🛡️ Security & Safeguards

  • All votes are recorded on BSC mainnet — immutable and verifiable by anyone
  • The 48-hour timelock ensures no surprise protocol changes
  • Major parameter changes (supply, fee >1%) require a 7-day vote period and 20% supermajority
  • Emergency multisig (3-of-5) can pause the timelock executor in case of exploits
  • No admin keys after the governance module launches in Phase 3
